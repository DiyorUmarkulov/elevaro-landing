Website redesign checklist: 12 things your business site must do in 2026

Most small business websites fail the same dozen checks. Run this list against yours — each miss is costing you customers you'll never know about.

April 14, 2026 · 7 min read

WebsitesConversion

Your website has one job: turn a visitor with intent into a call, booking, or order. Design awards don't matter; these twelve checks do.

Findability & speed

Loads in under three seconds on a phone. Passes Core Web Vitals. Has unique titles and descriptions on every page. Uses schema markup so search engines — and AI assistants — understand what you offer, where, and for whom.

Trust & conversion

Shows real photos, not stock. Displays reviews prominently. States prices or ranges where possible — hiding them loses more leads than it protects margin. Puts a phone number and primary call-to-action in the header, visible on every scroll.

The details most sites miss

A dedicated page per service and per location — one generic 'services' page can't rank for anything. Forms that ask three fields, not eleven. An SSL certificate and current copyright year, because visitors notice decay. And analytics that tie visits to actual calls and form fills.
