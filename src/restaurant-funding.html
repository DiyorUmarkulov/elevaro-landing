Funding options

Capital that fits the way you operate

Every restaurant is different. We match you with the structure that works for your revenue, timeline, and goals.

Revenue-based financing

Repay as a percentage of daily sales. When business is slow you pay less, when it picks up you pay more. No fixed monthly payments.

Merchant cash advances

Get a lump sum based on your card processing volume. Repayment is automatic through a small percentage of future card sales.

SBA & traditional loans

Lower rates and longer terms for established restaurants. We help you navigate the paperwork and connect with SBA-preferred lenders.

Equipment financing

Fund kitchen upgrades, POS systems, or buildouts with the equipment itself as collateral — often with no money down.

Lines of credit

Flexible capital you draw on when you need it — for inventory, payroll gaps, seasonal dips, or unexpected expenses.

Alternative & non-dilutive capital

Programs designed specifically for restaurants — including pre-sold dining credit, grant programs, and operator-friendly structures with no equity required.

FAQ

Common questions about restaurant funding

What kinds of restaurant funding can Elevaro help me find?

We connect you with a range of options: revenue-based financing, merchant cash advances, SBA loans, equipment financing, lines of credit, and alternative capital programs. We match based on your restaurant's profile, not a one-size-fits-all product.

Does Elevaro lend money directly?

No. Elevaro is a funding advisory — we connect you with vetted capital partners and help you compare terms. We don't lend, take equity, or charge application fees.

How much funding can I get?

It depends on your revenue, time in business, and credit profile. Most restaurant owners we work with secure between $10,000 and $500,000. Some programs serve larger multi-location groups as well.

How fast can I get funded?

Many of our partners fund within 3–10 business days after approval. SBA loans take longer — typically 30–90 days — but offer lower rates and longer terms.

Do I need perfect credit?

Not necessarily. Different programs have different requirements. Revenue-based financing and merchant cash advances focus more on your sales volume than your credit score. We'll match you with options that fit your situation.

What does it cost to apply through Elevaro?

Nothing. The consultation and matching process is free. You only pay the terms you agree to with the funding partner you choose.

Can I use the funding for anything?

In most cases, yes — renovations, new locations, equipment, marketing, hiring, inventory, or working capital. Some programs like equipment financing are tied to a specific purchase, but we'll explain the details upfront.
