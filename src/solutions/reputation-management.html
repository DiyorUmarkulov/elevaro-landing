Reputation

Common questions

Can you remove negative reviews from Google?

No one can guarantee removal of a legitimate negative review — be wary of anyone who claims otherwise. What we do: dispute reviews that violate platform policies (fake, spam, conflicts of interest), respond professionally to protect your reputation with future readers, and generate enough genuine positive reviews that one bad review stops defining you.

How do I get more Google reviews for my business?

Ask every happy customer, make it effortless with a direct review link, and time the request right after a positive experience. Elevaro automates this with SMS and email review campaigns that typically triple review velocity in the first 90 days.
