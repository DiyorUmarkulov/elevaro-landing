FAQ

Common questions about partnering

Who can become an Elevaro partner?

Anyone who works with or advises local businesses — agencies, consultants, SaaS platforms, accountants, franchisors, commercial real estate firms, POS companies, and more. If you regularly talk to business owners who need online presence help, there's a program that fits.

How much can I earn as a partner?

It depends on the program. Referral partners earn a commission on every client that signs — typically a percentage of the first-year contract value, paid monthly. Resellers set their own retail pricing above wholesale. Agency partners earn a revenue share on managed accounts. We share specific rates during onboarding.

Is there a cost to join the partner program?

No. There's no fee to apply, no setup cost, and no minimum volume requirement. You earn from day one of your first successful referral or resale.

Can I white-label Elevaro's services?

Yes. Resellers and agency partners can deliver under their own brand — white-labeled reports, portals, and communications. Your clients see your brand; we power the work behind the scenes.

What services can I offer through the partner program?

The full Elevaro catalog: business websites, Google Business Profile, local SEO, reputation management, social media, content marketing, e-commerce, analytics, and more. Partners can offer individual services or complete Growth, Scale, and Enterprise packages.

How do referral payouts work?

Referral commissions are tracked automatically via your unique partner link. Payouts are issued monthly once the referred client's first payment clears. You get real-time visibility into referral status in your partner dashboard.

Do I need technical expertise to resell?

No. Elevaro handles all delivery, technical setup, and ongoing management. You focus on the client relationship — we handle the work. Training resources and sales enablement materials are provided.

What if I already have clients who need these services?

Perfect — that's the fastest path to earning. Apply, get onboarded, and we'll help you transition existing clients or pitch new services to them. Many partners see their first commission within the first month.
