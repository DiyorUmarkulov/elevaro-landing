How should healthcare practices respond to negative reviews?

Never confirm the person was a patient or discuss care details — that risks privacy violations. Respond generically and professionally, invite the reviewer to contact the office directly, and focus on generating genuine positive reviews. Elevaro's healthcare response templates are built around these constraints.
