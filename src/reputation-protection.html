FAQ

Honest answers about reputation repair

Can a bad review really be removed?

Only if it violates platform policy — fake reviews, spam, conflicts of interest, hate speech, or personal data. We document violations and file disputes with the platforms. Legitimate negative reviews can't be removed by anyone; they're handled with professional responses and outweighed by genuine positive reviews.

How fast can you respond to a reputation crisis?

Monitoring alerts fire in near real time, urgent items are escalated the same day, and crisis responses are drafted within hours. If you're in an active situation right now, mention it in the form and we'll prioritize your request.

Is it legal to ask customers for reviews?

Yes — asking is fine and encouraged. What's prohibited is paying for reviews, offering incentives contingent on positive reviews, or review-gating (only asking happy customers through a filter). Our campaigns are fully compliant with platform policies and FTC guidance.
